## Executive Thesis

On **16 January 2024**, Synopsys agreed to acquire Ansys for approximately **$35 billion** in cash and stock—the largest technology transaction announced that year. Following an 18-month regulatory review across the United States, Europe, the United Kingdom, and China, the transaction closed on **17 July 2025**.

* **Industrial Logic:** Chip design and physical simulation historically operated as separate workflows. As AI processors moved into advanced 3D packaging and hyperscalers began designing custom silicon, thermal, power, and electromagnetic effects directly governed chip performance. Combining Ansys's multiphysics solvers with Synopsys's EDA platform expands the total addressable market by ~1.5x to **$28–31 billion** and establishes an end-to-end silicon-to-systems offering.
* **Structuring & Balance-Sheet Discipline:** Synopsys paired cash and stock with a hard **19.9999% share-issuance cap** to avoid a buyer shareholder vote, proactively offered targeted divestitures to clear global antitrust reviews, and raised **$14.3 billion** of investment-grade debt while committing to deleverage below **2.0x** within two years.

## Deal Mechanics & Regulatory Execution

* **19.9999% Share-Issuance Cap:** Kept newly issued shares strictly below NASDAQ's 20% threshold, eliminating the cost, delay, and deal-break risk of a separate Synopsys shareholder vote. At closing, the cap adjusted consideration to **$199.91** in cash and **0.3399** Synopsys shares per Ansys share.
* **Investment-Grade Financing & Deleveraging:** Funded via **$10.0 billion** in senior notes, a **$4.3 billion** prepayable term loan, and balance-sheet cash reinforced by the **$2.1 billion** sale of Synopsys's Software Integrity Group—preserving **Baa2 / BBB** ratings.
* **Antitrust Remedies:** Cleared multi-jurisdictional reviews (DOJ, EC, CMA, SAMR) by divesting Synopsys's **Optical Solutions Group** and Ansys's **PowerArtist** RTL power-analysis business to **Keysight Technologies** in October 2025.

## Key M&A Lessons for Deal Practitioners

1. **Proactive Remedies Preserve Deal Value:** Early, targeted divestitures of narrow product overlaps can clear multi-jurisdictional antitrust reviews without eroding the core strategic combination.
2. **Governance Caps Protect Speed:** Hard share-issuance caps (19.9999%) protect acquirer control and eliminate the execution overhang of a separate buyer shareholder vote.
3. **Recurring Cash Flow Supports IG Leverage:** High-multiple software acquisitions can maintain investment-grade credit ratings when backed by sticky recurring revenue and a credible two-year deleveraging path.
4. **Integration Drives Cross-Sell:** Cross-selling complementary engineering software only scales when solvers and workflows are deeply integrated at the product level rather than merely bundled commercially.
